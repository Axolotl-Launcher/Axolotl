# Axolotl Launcher 行为准则

## 我们的承诺

为了营造开放、友善和包容的社区环境，我们作为社区成员、贡献者和维护者承诺：无论参与者的年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同或表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济状况、国籍、外貌、种族、肤色、宗教、性取向或其他个人特征如何，都应获得不受骚扰的参与体验。

我们会以尊重、同理心和善意与他人交流，并共同维护一个健康的技术社区。

## 我们期望的行为

有助于营造积极环境的行为包括：

- 尊重不同的意见、观点和经历；
- 使用友善、包容且便于他人理解的语言；
- 给予并接受有建设性的反馈；
- 关注问题和解决方案，而不是针对个人；
- 承担责任，及时道歉并从错误中学习；
- 在不确定时先询问，而不是对他人作出未经证实的假设；
- 尊重他人的隐私、时间和沟通边界。

## 不可接受的行为

以下行为在社区中不可接受：

- 性骚扰，以及使用性化语言、图像或不受欢迎的性关注；
- 针对个人或群体的侮辱、贬损、嘲讽、挑衅、人身攻击或政治攻击；
- 跟踪、骚扰、威胁、故意曝光隐私或发布他人个人信息；
- 未经许可录音、截取或传播私人对话、账号信息或其他敏感内容；
- 以歧视性方式对待他人，或鼓励、合理化上述行为；
- 恶意刷屏、捣乱、冒充他人、散布恶意链接或其他蓄意破坏社区秩序的行为；
- 在专业场合中可能被合理视为不当的其他行为。

## 适用范围

本行为准则适用于 Axolotl Launcher 的所有官方社区空间，包括本仓库的 Issue、Pull Request、代码审查、讨论区、文档、官方账号和官方 QQ 群等。参与者在公开场合代表本项目时也应遵守本准则。

本项目使用的第三方平台可能另有自己的规则；遵守这些规则不能成为违反本行为准则的理由。本准则也不限制对代码、设计或项目决策进行善意、基于事实的批评。

## 执行与处理

项目维护者负责解释和执行本准则，并会根据事件的性质、影响和参与者的历史行为，采取公平、相称的措施。可能的措施包括：

1. **提醒或纠正**：指出不当行为并要求停止或修正；
2. **警告**：明确后果，并在必要时限制与相关人员或社区的互动；
3. **暂时限制参与**：在指定期限内限制发言、提交或参与官方社区空间；
4. **永久限制参与**：对于严重或持续的违规行为，永久禁止参与官方社区空间。

维护者可以移除、编辑或拒绝不符合本准则的评论、提交、Issue、Pull Request、代码、文档或其他贡献，并会在适当情况下说明处理原因。涉及安全、隐私或持续骚扰的事件，维护者可先采取临时措施，再完成调查。

## 如何报告问题

如果你遭遇或目睹了违反本准则的行为，请尽快联系项目维护者：

- 对于需要保密的事件，请在 [Axolotl GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Mystic-Stars/Axolotl/issues) 中仅留下“需要私下联系维护者”的简短说明，不要公开事件细节、个人信息、账号令牌或完整日志；维护者会通过可用的私下渠道与你联系。
- 对于一般性的社区问题，可以在相关 Issue 或官方 QQ 群（830619067）中提出。请勿在公开频道发布可能使当事人被识别的敏感信息。

维护者会及时、公平地受理和调查报告，并尽可能保护报告人的隐私。请仅提供与事件相关且你有权分享的信息；恶意或明显虚假的报告也可能构成对社区的骚扰。

## 归属与许可

本行为准则改编自 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html)。如需了解该模板的常见问题，请参阅 [Contributor Covenant FAQ](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/faq)。

# Axolotl Launcher Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In order to foster an open, friendly, and inclusive community environment, we, as community members, contributors, and maintainers, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, color, religion, sexual orientation, or any other personal characteristic.

We will interact with others with respect, empathy, and kindness, and together maintain a healthy technology community.

## Expected Behavior

Behaviors that help foster a positive environment include:

- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- Using language that is friendly, inclusive, and easy for others to understand;
- Give and receive constructive feedback;
- Focus on issues and solutions, not on individuals;
- Take responsibility, apologize promptly, and learn from mistakes;
- When uncertain, ask first rather than making unverified assumptions about others;
- Respect others' privacy, time, and communication boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable in the community:

- Sexual harassment, as well as the use of sexualized language, images, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- Insults, denigration, mockery, provocation, personal attacks, or political attacks against individuals or groups;
- Stalking, harassment, threats, intentional exposure of private information, or publishing others' personal information;
- Recording, intercepting, or disseminating private conversations, account information, or other sensitive content without permission;
- Treating others in a discriminatory manner, or encouraging or rationalizing the above behaviors;
- Engaging in malicious spamming, disruption, impersonating others, spreading malicious links, or other actions that intentionally undermine community order;
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official community spaces of Axolotl Launcher, including Issues, Pull Requests, code reviews, discussion forums, documentation, official accounts, and official QQ groups in this repository. Participants should also adhere to this Code of Conduct when representing the project in public.

Third-party platforms used by this project may have their own rules; compliance with those rules does not constitute a valid reason for violating this Code of Conduct. This Code also does not restrict good-faith, fact-based criticism of code, design, or project decisions.

## Enforcement and Handling

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct and will take fair and proportionate measures based on the nature of the incident, its impact, and the prior conduct of the participants involved. Possible measures include:

1. **Reminder or Correction**: Point out the inappropriate behavior and require it to be stopped or corrected.
2. **Warning**: Clearly state the consequences and, if necessary, restrict interaction with the involved parties or the community;
3. **Temporary Restriction from Participation**: Restrict speaking, submitting, or participating in official community spaces for a specified period;
4. **Permanent Restriction from Participation**: Permanently prohibit participation in official community spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, code, documentation, or other contributions that do not align with these guidelines, and will explain the reasons for their actions when appropriate. For incidents involving security, privacy, or ongoing harassment, maintainers may take temporary measures before completing an investigation.

## How to Report Issues

If you experience or witness a violation of this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers as soon as possible:

- For confidential matters, please leave only a brief note saying "Need to contact maintainers privately" in the [Axolotl GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Mystic-Stars/Axolotl/issues), without publicly disclosing event details, personal information, account tokens, or full logs; the maintainers will contact you through available private channels.
- For general community issues, you can raise them in the relevant Issue or the official QQ group (830619067). Do not post sensitive information in public channels that could identify the parties involved.

Maintainers will promptly and fairly receive and investigate reports, and protect the privacy of reporters to the best of their ability. Please only provide information that is relevant to the incident and that you are authorized to share; malicious or clearly false reports may also constitute harassment of the community.
